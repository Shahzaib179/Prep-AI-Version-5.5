# Prep AI v5.3

- **Profile menu:** the user button in the top bar opens a popover: name, student ID (students) or "Tutor account" (tutors), email, change password, log out (`profile_pages.py`). The sign-in caption, change-password box and Log out button were removed from the sidebar for students and tutors.
- **Today's Finish Line:** exactly four items: Answer N questions, Revise due topics, Retake mistakes, Daily Challenge. Revise / Retake count as done when nothing is due or open (the card says "Nothing due today"). Study-plan tick boxes moved out.
- **Today's Schedule:** study-plan sessions (tick boxes) and tutor assignments / quizzes (class, quiz or PDF, due date, overdue / due-soon badge).
- **Alignment:** both cards of a row share one height via `st.container(height=...)` (`ROW1_H`, `ROW2_H`, `ROW3_H` in dashboard_pages.py); longer content scrolls inside its card. Row 1: Finish Line | Schedule. Row 2: Score estimate | Strengths & weaknesses | Next step. Row 3: Games | Leaderboard. The "My goal" card was removed (Edit goal stays in the sidebar).
- **Quick MCQ Challenge:** pick 5-20 MCQs with a slider; one question at a time, 5 seconds each; an unanswered question is skipped and the next appears.
- **Rapid Fire** (replaces Subject Sprint): 60 seconds, as many MCQs as you can; only answered questions are recorded. The pool is up to 40 real questions (bank + starter set, no AI wait); if you finish them all early the round ends and says so.
- **Timing:** the countdown is a fragment that reruns every 0.5 s. The server clock decides: an answer arriving more than 0.8 s after its deadline is not counted. A timed-out Quick question can stay on screen up to about half a second longer.
- Old v5.2 "Subject Sprint" results stay in history under that name.
- **Bug fix found while testing:** the dashboard's study-plan "today" compared a UTC creation date with the local (UTC+5) day, so between 00:00 and 05:00 Pakistan time the plan showed the next day's sessions. Fixed in `dashboard_data.plan_today`.
